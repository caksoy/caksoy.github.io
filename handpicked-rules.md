## Community Rules

The Handpicked community is united by one thing: recommending movies to strangers. Together we are building a single deck of movie cards for everyone, from people with different tastes and different points of view.

Every card in Handpicked is one person saying *watch this*. Once it is in the deck, strangers see it under a random name, and no one can reply to it. These rules are about what a card may not say.

We don’t allow cards that contain any of the following:

- **Hate or Harassment.** Insulting, humiliating or threatening a person or a group, degrading people for who they are, or attacking a religion.
- **Violent or Disturbing Content.** Graphic descriptions of killing, torture or abuse, or encouraging or glorifying violence, terrorism, suicide or self-harm.
- **Sexual Content.** Explicit sexual descriptions, anything that sexualizes minors, and movies that TMDB flags as adult.
- **Dangerous or Illegal Activity.** Promoting anything illegal or dangerous.
- **Personal Information.** Someone’s private contact or location details.
- **Spam, Ads or Links.** Advertising, asking for money or contact, and any links.
- **Political Campaigning.** Endorsing or attacking a political figure, party or cause.

Every card is checked automatically after you post it. A card that breaks the rules is not accepted and never enters the deck. You will see it marked on your cards, and you can delete it and try again.

Cards can also be reported by anyone who sees them in the deck. We read every report. If the card breaks the rules, we remove it from the deck and from the watchlists of everyone who added it, and mark it on your cards.

If you see a card that breaks these rules, report it from the menu on the card. You can also block the person who posted it, and their cards leave your deck. You can unblock them any time.

We update these rules as we learn what the deck needs. This page is always the current version.

**Contact:** You can reach us at [handpickdmovies@gmail.com](mailto:handpickdmovies@gmail.com).
