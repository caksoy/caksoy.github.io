## Privacy Policy

Handpicked has no sign-up. Your account is created the first time you open the app, with a random name, and we never ask for your email address or your name. The app still needs to know a few things about you to work, and this page says what they are, who else is involved, and how long we keep them.

### What we collect

To keep your account with you, your phone sends us three things:

- **Your iCloud record.** If you are signed into iCloud, Apple gives Handpicked a user record: a code made for this app only, which tells us nothing about your Apple account, so your account is the same on all your devices.
- **A code for your phone.** A random code your phone makes, so we recognise it next time.
- **A key for your phone.** Your phone proves it is running the real app through Apple’s App Attest, which gives us a key for that phone.

And you get a random name.

Once you use the app, we keep what you do in it:

- **Your cards.** The movie, your pitch and when you posted it.
- **Your watchlist.** Which cards you added, and when you removed one.
- **What you have seen.** Which cards the deck has shown you, so it does not repeat them and each card counts you once.
- **Your numbers.** How many people saw each of your cards and how many added it to their watchlist.
- **Reports and blocks.** The cards you reported and why, and the people you blocked.

If you pay once to support Handpicked, the purchase is handled by Apple on your phone. We never see your payment details and keep no record of who has paid. Your account and your data stay exactly the same.

We use all of this only to run the app. Handpicked has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell your data.

### Who else is involved

A few outside services make the app work. Here is what each one gets.

- **OpenAI.** Every pitch is checked automatically against the community rules by OpenAI’s API. Only the text of the pitch is sent, with no name and no identifier.
- **TMDB.** Movie details and posters come from TMDB, and streaming availability from JustWatch through TMDB. When you search for a movie, our server sends TMDB your search text. Nothing about you reaches it.
- **Apple.** Your iCloud record, App Attest and the supporter purchase all go through Apple, under Apple’s own privacy policy.
- **Amazon Web Services and Cloudflare.** Our server and database run on AWS in the United States. Cloudflare sits in front of the server and delivers the posters. Like any website, both see your phone’s IP address when it connects.

Each of these services is bound by its own privacy policy, and each one gets only what is listed here.

### How long we keep it

Everything in "What we collect" stays as long as your account does. Our server also keeps a log of each request for 30 days: the time, what was asked for, which account asked and your phone’s IP address. Errors are recorded for the same 30 days. Backups of the database are kept for up to 56 days.

To delete your account, open More and tap Delete account.

- **Deleted at once.** Your iCloud record if you have one, your phone’s code and key, your cards, your watchlist, what you have seen, your reports and your blocks. If someone had added one of your cards, the movie stays on their watchlist, with no pitch and no name.
- **What we keep.** Your old username, so it is never given to anyone else, and a fingerprint of your phone’s key, not the key itself, so the app on your phone knows the account is gone.
- **Backups.** Copies in backups disappear within 56 days.
- **If you paid to support Handpicked.** Your purchase is not affected. It belongs to your Apple ID, not to your Handpicked account.

We update this page when the app changes. This page is always the current version.
