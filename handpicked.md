## Handpicked

Looking for a movie, but tired of scrolling lists and reading threads? What you want is a person saying *watch this*. Handpicked is full of them.

One deck of cards. Each card is a movie someone posted, with a pitch on why you should watch it. The deck is shuffled, not ranked: no algorithm decides what comes next, and no one is louder than anyone else.

Going through the deck is simple and fast. Swipe right for the next card, left to go back. When a pitch convinces you, swipe up to add the movie to your watchlist.

Got a movie you keep telling people about? Pick it, say why, post. It’s in the deck for everyone.

No likes, no followers, no profiles. Everyone gets a random name.

The only numbers in Handpicked are on your own cards: how many people saw each one, and how many added it to their watchlist thanks to you.

Handpicked is free, with no ads and no subscription. If you like it, you can pay once to support it. Nothing in the app changes.

Handpicked is made for iPhone and needs iOS 18.1 or later.

Movie details and posters come from TMDB. Streaming availability comes from JustWatch.
